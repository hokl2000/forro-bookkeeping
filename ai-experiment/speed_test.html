Can this computer run the AI model?

A speed test for the Forró bookkeeping AI experiment. It loads an open-source language model (Qwen3) into this browser with WebLLM and sends it dummy prompts of growing length. No data is used and nothing is uploaded. The first time, the model is downloaded and kept in the browser's cache; later loads are much faster.

The real prompts in the experiment are up to about 8,600 characters, so the 8,000-character step is the one that matters.
