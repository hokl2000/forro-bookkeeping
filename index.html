Getting ready … (the first time takes about 10 seconds)

Continue where you left off (optional)

Did you save your work with Save progress last time? Add that file here and the bookings come back as you left them. Then you don't need steps 1–3.

Step 1 – Bank statement

In the bank, open the club's account, choose 1 August – 31 July and click Eksporter til CSV. Don't open the file in Excel first.

Step 2 – MobilePay

In MobilePay, download the transactions report (CSV) from 24 July (one week before the financial year starts) to 31 July the year after. The extra week catches payments from late July that reach the bank in early August. Don't open the file in Excel first.

Step 3 – Zettle

Log in to Zettle, go to Udbetalinger (menu on the left), choose 24 July – 31 July the year after and click the button Eksporter til Excel.
